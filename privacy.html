Privacy Policy

This policy describes how the AppSphere portfolio website handles information. AppSphere is a static project showcase; it does not provide user accounts or a server-side contact form.

Information and preferences

AppSphere does not ask you to submit personal information to use the portfolio. The login and registration panels are demonstrations only. Their values are checked by your browser, are not transmitted to AppSphere, and are not saved by the site.

The site stores a small number of preferences in your browser's local storage: your selected display theme, repositories you have starred in the interface, and your choice about optional analytics. These are not browser cookies and are not sent to the site. You can clear them using your browser's site-data controls.

Cookies and analytics

AppSphere does not currently set optional analytics or advertising cookies, and analytics tracking is disabled. The privacy-preferences banner records whether you accepted or rejected future optional analytics in local storage; selecting accept does not activate a tracker while none is configured. You can change this choice at any time using cookie preferences.

Hosting and third-party services

When you load this static site, your browser requests files from the hosting provider and the third-party font and icon CDNs referenced by the page. Those providers may receive technical connection data such as your IP address under their own privacy terms. Links to GitHub, project demos, and app releases take you to services with their own policies. AppSphere does not control those services.

Children and changes

This portfolio is not designed to collect information from children. This policy may be updated when the site adds or changes functionality. The effective date above will be revised when that happens.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to tanujdarokar@gmail.com.
