How to Install Android APKs

A simple 3-step guide to installing independent apps on your Android phone or tablet.

1

Download the APK file

Click any "Download APK" button on this site. Your browser (Chrome, Samsung Internet, Firefox, etc.) will start saving the .apk package to your Downloads folder.

2

Enable "Install Unknown Apps"

When opening the downloaded file, Android may display a security prompt: "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source."

Tap Settings on the prompt & toggle "Allow from this source" to ON.
3

Tap "Install" & Launch

Press your back button to return to the installer dialog and tap Install. Once completed, tap Open to start using your new Flutter app!

Is it safe to install these APKs?
Yes. These APKs are compiled directly from source code repositories with standard debug/release Android signatures and contain zero spyware or malicious code.
How do I update to newer versions?
Simply download the updated APK from this site and install it over the previous version. Your app data and settings will be preserved automatically.
